How this episode was made a Bruin pipeline, start to finish

  1. Today's news. Telem searches several providers at once for fresh AI and surveillance headlines and reads the best articles in full.

  3. Quality gates. Bruin checks block rendering if a real name leaks into the script, a path dead-ends, a line runs long, or the critic's score is too low.
